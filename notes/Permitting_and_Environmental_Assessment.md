## Notes

- **Visual characterization of anti-reflective coating on solar module glass** — Karin & Jain (2020), *IEEE*. Method for judging AR coating condition on module glass visually — relevant both to soiling assessment and to the reflectivity numbers a glare study rests on. Keywords: anti-reflective coating, module glass, visual inspection, reflectivity. Related: [[PV_Degradation_and_Reliability]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/94BWASTT)
- **[General Design Procedures for Airport-Based Solar Photovoltaic Systems](https://doi.org/10.3390/en10081194)** — Anurag et al. (2017), *Energies*. Works through the three FAA objections to PV at airports — reflectivity and glare, radar interference, and airspace penetration — and what a design has to demonstrate on each. The clearest treatment of glare as a permitting constraint rather than a performance one. Keywords: glare, airports, FAA, radar interference, reflectivity. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/RLQP389Y)
- **[Icke-bindande vägledning för god praxis vid tillämpningen av direktiv 2013/35/EU elektromagnetiska fält, Volym 1](https://data.europa.eu/doi/10.2767/9804)** (Non-binding guide to good practice for implementing the EMF Directive 2013/35/EU, Volume 1: practical guidance) — European Commission (2015), *Publications Office*. The practical guide behind the EU workplace EMF limits: how to assess exposure and what counts as compliance. The reference for the EMF section of an inverter-station assessment. Keywords: EMF directive, exposure limits, workplace assessment, 2013/35/EU. Related: [[PV_Electrical_Design_and_Inverters]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/F6QDP2SK) #region/europe #type/regulation
- **[Electromagnetic Fields Associated with Commercial Solar Photovoltaic Electric Power Generating Facilities](https://doi.org/10.1080/15459624.2015.1047021)** — Tell et al. (2015), *Journal of Occupational and Environmental Hygiene*. Measured electric and magnetic fields from 0 Hz to 3 GHz at two Southern California Edison plants, across the inverter and step-up transformer chain. The measurement study to cite when EMF is raised at samråd. Keywords: EMF measurement, inverters, transformers, exposure guidelines. Related: [[PV_Electrical_Design_and_Inverters]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ZJEUHWCP)
- **[Natura 2000: protecting Europe's biodiversity](https://data.europa.eu/doi/10.2779/45963)** — Directorate-General for Environment (2008), *Publications Office of the European Union*. How the Natura 2000 network is constituted and what designation means in practice across member states — the background to the habitats screening any Nordic site needs. Keywords: Natura 2000, habitats directive, designation, biodiversity. Related: [[Geodata_Portals_Nordic]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ERVHHT6F) #region/europe #project/thesis
- **Natura 2000 in the Boreal region** — Sundseth (2006), *Office for Official Publications of the European Communities*. The boreal-region volume: which habitat and species types drive designation at Nordic latitudes. Keywords: Natura 2000, boreal region, habitat types, Nordic. Related: [[Swedish_Forestry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/J7CSPG8W) #region/nordic #project/thesis
- **[Elektromagnetiska fält — Arbetsmiljöverket](https://www.av.se/halsa-och-sakerhet/elektromagnetiska-falt/)** (Electromagnetic fields — Swedish Work Environment Authority) — Arbetsmiljöverket, n.d. The Swedish authority's position: EMF is present at nearly every workplace but normally low enough to rule out health effects, with direct effects appearing only around large currents or high voltages. Keywords: EMF, workplace, Arbetsmiljöverket, Swedish guidance. Related: [[PV_Electrical_Design_and_Inverters]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/6INJASH3) #region/sweden #type/regulation
- **[Energimyndigheten](https://www.energimyndigheten.se/)** — Swedish Energy Agency, n.d. The agency behind Solelportalen and the national guidance on solcellsparker, and the usual starting point for what the state expects of a Swedish solar development. Keywords: Energimyndigheten, Solelportalen, national guidance, solcellsparker. Related: [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]], [[Energy_Transition_and_Scenarios]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/U24TU3QG) #region/sweden #type/portal
- **[Research and Analysis Demonstrate the Lack of Impacts of Glare from Photovoltaic Modules](https://www.nrel.gov/state-local-tribal/blog/posts/research-and-analysis-demonstrate-the-lack-of-impacts-of-glare-from-photovoltaic-modules.html)** — Mow, NREL, n.d. Short, citable answer to the standard local objection: PV modules glare less than the windows and water already in the landscape. Useful in a samråd response. Keywords: glare, public objections, NREL, siting. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/IB2NZF7C) #type/reference
