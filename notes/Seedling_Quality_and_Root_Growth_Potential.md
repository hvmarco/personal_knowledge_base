# Seedling Quality and Root Growth Potential

**Summary**: Judging whether a seedling will perform after planting — root growth potential and capacity, the target seedling concept, vigour and quality tests, and how well any of them actually predict field performance.
**Parent**: [[Forestry]] · **Related**: [[Forest_Seedling_Production]], [[Seedling_Storage_and_Handling]], [[Forest_Regeneration]]

## Notes

- **[Supplemental But Not Photoperiodic Lighting Increased Seedling Quality and Reduced Production Time](https://doi.org/10.21273/HORTSCI13664-18)** — Hurt et al. (2019), *HortScience*. Separates the two things nursery lighting is used for and finds only the supplemental-light effect improves quality and shortens the crop. Keywords: supplemental lighting, photoperiodic lighting, seedling quality, production time. Related: [[Lighting_Strategy_and_Control]], [[Forest_Seedling_Production]].
