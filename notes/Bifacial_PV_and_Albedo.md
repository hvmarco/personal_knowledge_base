## Notes

- [MODIS Web](https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/mod43.php) — NASA's product page for the MOD43/MCD43 BRDF-albedo product family, the source description behind the MCD43 albedo data used elsewhere on this page. Keywords: MODIS, MOD43, BRDF, albedo product. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. #type/dataset
- [LP DAAC - MCD43C3](https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/products/mcd43c3v061/) — LP DAAC product page for MCD43C3, the coarse (0.05°) climate-modelling-grid albedo product — easier to pull globally than the tiled MCD43A3. Keywords: MCD43C3, LP DAAC, albedo, climate modelling grid. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. #type/dataset
- [Surface albedo 10-daily gridded data from 1981 to present](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/cdsapp#!/dataset/satellite-albedo) — Copernicus Climate Data Store entry for a 10-daily gridded surface-albedo product running from 1981 — a longer, consistent alternative to MODIS-only records. Keywords: CDS, surface albedo, gridded, long time series. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. #type/dataset
- [Albedo Data and Surface Reflectance Data Visualizations in SNAP using MODIS Imagery - CUOSGwiki](https://dges.carleton.ca/CUOSGwiki/index.php/Albedo_Data_and_Surface_Reflectance_Data_Visualizations_in_SNAP_using_MODIS_Imagery) — Walkthrough of visualising albedo and surface-reflectance data from MODIS imagery inside ESA's SNAP toolbox. Keywords: SNAP, MODIS, albedo visualization, CUOSGwiki. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. #type/dataset
- [bifacial_radiance](https://bifacial-radiance.readthedocs.io/en/latest/) — Documentation for NREL's bifacial_radiance, the ray-tracing package for simulating bifacial PV yield under realistic 3D scenes. Keywords: bifacial_radiance, NREL, ray tracing, documentation. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. #type/tool
- [Bifacial_Radiance Software Training – Part 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1X9L-R-RVGA) — First video in a recorded bifacial_radiance training series: setup and the first ray-trace run. Keywords: bifacial_radiance, training video, ray tracing, part 1. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. #type/video
- [NREL/bifacial_radiance: 1 - Introductory Example - Fixed Tilt simple setup.ipynb](https://github.com/NREL/bifacial_radiance/blob/master/docs/tutorials/1%20-%20Introductory%20Example%20-%20Fixed%20Tilt%20simple%20setup.ipynb) — The package's own introductory tutorial notebook: a minimal fixed-tilt bifacial simulation from scratch. Keywords: bifacial_radiance, tutorial notebook, fixed tilt, introductory example. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. #type/tool
- **[Spatial and Seasonal Variation of Albedo Exhibited by Different Land Cover Across Sweden](https://cms.it.gu.se/infoglueDeliverWorking/digitalAssets/1788/1788134_taybeh.pdf)** — Kheirkhah (2020), MSc thesis. Partitions albedo by land-cover class (bare soil, grassland, wetland, cropland, forest) across five latitudinal sites from Vambåsa to Kardis for 2017 — the ground-cover numbers a Swedish bifacial yield estimate needs. Keywords: albedo, land cover, latitudinal gradient, Sweden, seasonal variation. Related: [[PV_in_Nordic_Climates]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/9Z29MKZA) #region/sweden
- **[Experimental Yield Study of Bifacial PV Modules in Nordic Conditions](https://doi.org/10.1109/JPHOTOV.2018.2865168)** — Molin et al. (2018), *IEEE Journal of Photovoltaics*. First full-year Swedish field study, on two flat roofs at Linköping (58°N) with albedo 0.05: bifacial gain was only 5% at 40° tilt and 1% for vertical east–west modules, and the power temperature coefficients differ from monofacial. The low-albedo reality check. Keywords: bifacial gain, Nordic field study, vertical east-west, temperature coefficient. Related: [[PV_in_Nordic_Climates]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/HMVU645F) #region/sweden #region/nordic
- **[Simulation of Bifacial PV Modules in Nordic Conditions for Low and High Albedo](https://doi.org/10.4229/35thEUPVSEC20182018-6BO.8.6)** — Molin et al. (2018), *35th EU PVSEC*. The modelling companion to the Linköping field study, spanning low to high albedo so the snow-covered and bare-roof cases can be compared. Keywords: bifacial simulation, albedo sensitivity, Nordic conditions. Related: [[PV_in_Nordic_Climates]], [[PV_Modelling_Tools]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/M7Q7SZXD) #region/nordic
- **[Key Elements in the Design of Bifacial PV Power Plants](https://doi.org/10.4229/EUPVSEC20152015-5CO.14.4)** — Radouane et al. (2015), *EU PVSEC*. What changes when a utility-scale plant is bifacial: ground treatment, mounting height and row pitch, and the design decisions that follow from rear-side gain. Keywords: plant design, mounting height, ground cover, bifacial plants. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ASC5IJ9D)
