## Notes

- **[Spatial and Seasonal Variation of Albedo Exhibited by Different Land Cover Across Sweden](https://cms.it.gu.se/infoglueDeliverWorking/digitalAssets/1788/1788134_taybeh.pdf)** — Kheirkhah (2020), MSc thesis. Partitions albedo by land-cover class (bare soil, grassland, wetland, cropland, forest) across five latitudinal sites from Vambåsa to Kardis for 2017 — the ground-cover numbers a Swedish bifacial yield estimate needs. Keywords: albedo, land cover, latitudinal gradient, Sweden, seasonal variation. Related: [[PV_in_Nordic_Climates]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/9Z29MKZA) #region/sweden
- **[Experimental Yield Study of Bifacial PV Modules in Nordic Conditions](https://doi.org/10.1109/JPHOTOV.2018.2865168)** — Molin et al. (2018), *IEEE Journal of Photovoltaics*. First full-year Swedish field study, on two flat roofs at Linköping (58°N) with albedo 0.05: bifacial gain was only 5% at 40° tilt and 1% for vertical east–west modules, and the power temperature coefficients differ from monofacial. The low-albedo reality check. Keywords: bifacial gain, Nordic field study, vertical east-west, temperature coefficient. Related: [[PV_in_Nordic_Climates]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/HMVU645F) #region/sweden #region/nordic
- **[Simulation of Bifacial PV Modules in Nordic Conditions for Low and High Albedo](https://doi.org/10.4229/35thEUPVSEC20182018-6BO.8.6)** — Molin et al. (2018), *35th EU PVSEC*. The modelling companion to the Linköping field study, spanning low to high albedo so the snow-covered and bare-roof cases can be compared. Keywords: bifacial simulation, albedo sensitivity, Nordic conditions. Related: [[PV_in_Nordic_Climates]], [[PV_Modelling_Tools]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/M7Q7SZXD) #region/nordic
- **[Key Elements in the Design of Bifacial PV Power Plants](https://doi.org/10.4229/EUPVSEC20152015-5CO.14.4)** — Radouane et al. (2015), *EU PVSEC*. What changes when a utility-scale plant is bifacial: ground treatment, mounting height and row pitch, and the design decisions that follow from rear-side gain. Keywords: plant design, mounting height, ground cover, bifacial plants. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ASC5IJ9D)
