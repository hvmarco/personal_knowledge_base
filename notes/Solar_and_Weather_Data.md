# Solar and Weather Data

**Summary**: Solar and meteorological data sources — national observation networks, satellite-derived irradiance services, radiation databases and the papers that validate them.

## Notes

- **[JRC Photovoltaic Geographical Information System (PVGIS 5.1)](https://ec.europa.eu/jrc/en/pvgis)** — European Commission JRC (2021). The free European irradiance and PV-yield service, with an API. Default first look for any European site before commercial data is bought. Keywords: PVGIS, API, irradiance, PV yield, free. Related: [[PV_Modelling_Tools]], [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ABMALLRX) #type/api #region/europe
- **[Solcast: Validation of a satellite-derived solar irradiance dataset](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2019.07.086)** — Bright (2019), *Solar Energy*. Independent validation of Solcast's hourly and sub-hourly GHI against 48 BSRN ground stations — over a million hourly and nearly three million instantaneous comparisons. The evidence base for trusting the dataset. Keywords: Solcast, validation, BSRN, GHI, satellite-derived. Related: [[PV_Forecasting_and_Ramp_Control]], [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/U4LHCSWI) #type/dataset
- **[Wind and solar resource data sets](https://doi.org/10.1002/wene.276)** — Clifton et al. (2018), *WIREs Energy and Environment*. Reviews the whole range from static mean-annual cartography to high-resolution products, and how each is created and validated. The orientation piece for choosing a resource dataset. Keywords: resource datasets, validation methods, review, prospecting. Related: [[Global_Datasets_and_Catalogs]], [[Energy_Transition_and_Scenarios]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/328P62TM)
- **[MeteoNorm](http://www.meteonorm.com/en/)** — Meteotest (2016). Commercial climatological database and TMY generator giving irradiation, temperature and further parameters for any location. The usual input to PVsyst. Keywords: Meteonorm, TMY, climatological database, interpolation. Related: [[PV_Modelling_Tools]], [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/BJAJ5R2T) #type/tool #project/thesis
- **[Typical Meteorological Year Data: SolarGIS Approach](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.egypro.2015.03.195)** — Cebecauer & Šúri (2015), *Energy Procedia*. What actually goes into constructing a TMY — input period, frequency and quality, parameter weighting and coherence, selection criteria — and how to build P50 versus P90 years. Read before accepting any TMY at face value. Keywords: TMY construction, P50, P90, uncertainty, selection criteria. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]], [[Statistics_and_Data_Analysis]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/IDSKGUUG)
- **[A new solar radiation database for estimating PV performance in Europe and Africa](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2012.03.006)** — Huld et al. (2012), *Solar Energy*. Documents the move of PVGIS onto CM-SAF satellite-derived radiation, which brought very low overall bias. The reference for what PVGIS's European numbers rest on. Keywords: PVGIS, CM-SAF, satellite radiation, bias, Europe and Africa. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/DGIW8CMF) #region/europe #project/light-model
- **[Energy & Weather Services](http://www.meteocontrol.de/)** — Meteocontrol (2012). Commercial monitoring and weather-service provider for PV fleets. Keywords: monitoring service, weather data, fleet. Related: [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/PYGHKWUF) #type/portal
- **SolarGIS: Solar data and online applications for PV planning and performance assessment** — Šúri et al. (2011). Introduces the SolarGIS database and its online planning and performance tools. Keywords: SolarGIS, planning tools, performance assessment. Related: [[PV_Modelling_Tools]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ER3UCHLV) #type/dataset
- **[Online data and tools for estimation of solar electricity in Africa: the PVGIS approach](https://publications.jrc.ec.europa.eu/repository/handle/JRC30889)** — Šúri et al. (2006), *WIP-Renewable Energies*. The African and Mediterranean extension, built on Meteosat imagery processed by Heliosat-2 into HelioClim-1. Keywords: PVGIS Africa, Heliosat-2, HelioClim, Meteosat. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/9UEAVQ3C)
- **[PV-GIS: a web-based solar radiation database for the calculation of PV potential in Europe](https://doi.org/10.1080/14786450512331329556)** — Šúri et al. (2005), *International Journal of Sustainable Energy*. The original PVGIS paper: a GIS-held European radiation database with monthly and yearly global irradiation on horizontal and inclined surfaces, plus three interactive web applications. Keywords: PVGIS origin, GIS database, PV potential, web application. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]], [[Geospatial_Python]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/GDDAQ342) #region/europe
- **[STRÅNG — a system for modelling solar radiation parameters with mesoscale spatial resolution](http://strang.smhi.se/)** — Landelius et al. (2001), *SMHI*. The Swedish mesoscale radiation model: global, direct, diffuse and PAR on an hourly grid back to the 1990s, free to download. The default Swedish irradiance source when no on-site measurement exists. Keywords: STRÅNG, SMHI, mesoscale, PAR, Sweden. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]], [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/SMGYT6RG) #region/sweden #type/dataset #project/thesis
- **[Satel-Light: The European Database of Daylight and Solar Radiation, 1996–1998](http://www.satel-light.com/core.htm)** — Satel-Light (1996). Early Meteosat-derived European daylight and irradiance database. Largely historical now, but the ancestor of the satellite-derived services. Keywords: Satel-Light, daylight, Meteosat, Europe. Related: [[Radiometry_and_Photometry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/YG4DJL9P) #region/europe
- **[NREL MIDC: Solar Radiation Research Laboratory (BMS)](http://www.nrel.gov/midc/apps/go2url.pl?site=BMS&page=day.pl?BMS)** — Andreas, NREL. High-quality reference irradiance and meteorological measurements from NREL's Measurement and Instrumentation Data Center — the sort of ground truth satellite products get validated against. Keywords: MIDC, BMS, reference measurements, ground truth. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/YFYWCWCB) #type/dataset
- **[Augusti 2014 — Extremt regnig i västra Götaland | SMHI](https://www.smhi.se/klimat/klimatet-da-och-nu/manadens-vader-och-vatten-sverige/manadens-vader-i-sverige/augusti-2014-extremt-regnig-i-vastra-gotaland-1.76746)** (August 2014 — extremely rainy in western Götaland) — SMHI. SMHI's account of an extreme-rainfall month; the kind of event record behind skyfall statistics used in stormwater design. Keywords: extreme rainfall, skyfall, August 2014, Västra Götaland. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]], [[Climate_Change]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/3NLPTNJ6) #region/sweden
- **[Surface meteorology and Solar Energy](https://eosweb.larc.nasa.gov/sse/)** — NASA. Global meteorology and surface solar climatology from NASA satellite data on a 1° grid — coarse, but free and global, and the predecessor of NASA POWER. Keywords: NASA SSE, global climatology, 1-degree grid, POWER. Related: [[Global_Datasets_and_Catalogs]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/QSBBXN9R) #type/dataset #region/global
- **[The NSRDB Data Viewer](https://maps.nrel.gov/nsrdb-viewer/#/?aL=e8KZAu%255Bv%255D%3Dt&bL=groad&cE=0&lR=0&mC=27.566721430409707%2C-87.275390625&zL=4)** — NREL.
