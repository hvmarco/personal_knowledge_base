## Notes

- [Handel och prissättning (Trading and pricing) — Svenska kraftnät](https://www.svk.se/aktorsportalen/bidra-med-reserver/handel-prissattning/) — how Sweden's TSO trades and prices reserves in the Nordic balancing market. Covers six products (FCR-N, FCR-D up/down, aFRR, mFRR, FFR) and their procurement timelines: FCR and aFRR on D-1 auctions, mFRR capacity day-ahead with energy bids 45 min before the operating hour, FFR annually. Marginal pricing for FCR and aFRR capacity, pay-as-bid for mFRR capacity, energy settled on Nord Pool up/down-regulation prices; capacity payment = price × MW, energy payment = price × MWh. Historical prices via Mimer and eSett, market surveillance under REMIT. Keywords: ancillary services, frequency reserves, FCR/aFRR/mFRR, balancing market, capacity pricing.
