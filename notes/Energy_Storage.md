## Notes

- **[Reducing the Impact of Forest Plant Production — Design of a Stand-Alone PV-Hybrid System for Powering an Innovative Forestry Incubator](https://doi.org/10.4229/EUPVSEC20142014-6AV.5.28)** — Pamidi & Hernandez Velasco (2014), *EU PVSEC*. Sizes a stand-alone PV-hybrid supply for a forestry seedling incubator, so nursery propagation can run off-grid. Part of the ZEPHYR work. Keywords: stand-alone PV, hybrid system, forestry incubator, off-grid sizing. Related: [[Forest_Seedling_Production]], [[Horticultural_Lighting]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/6FGMHS5Y) #project/zephyr #project/own-publications
- **[Energy Storage for a Grid-Connected PV-System: A Feasibility Study](http://www.diva-portal.org/smash/record.jsf?pid=diva2%3A642696)** — Pérez de la Mora (2013), MSc thesis. Dimensions a storage system as an energy buffer for a grid-connected PV plant, and asks what it would have to cost to be worth installing. Keywords: storage sizing, grid-connected PV, buffer, feasibility. Related: [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/PBURNGD7)
- **Secondary cells and batteries for photovoltaic energy systems (PVES) — General requirements and methods of test** — IEC 61427 (2005). The battery standard for PV: what is required of cells in PV service and the test methods used to verify it. Keywords: IEC 61427, battery testing, PVES, requirements. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/DFDRR7WW) #type/regulation #project/msc-thesis/4-1
- **[Secondary cells and batteries for photovoltaic energy systems (PVES)](https://doi.org/10.1109/INTLEC.2005.335175)** — Lenain (2005), *INTELEC*. Walks through IEC 61427 Ed. 2 for lead-acid and nickel-cadmium batteries, explaining the purpose of each test so a specifier can match requirement to duty cycle. Keywords: IEC 61427, lead-acid, nickel-cadmium, specification. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/8MLV9GDD)
- **[Methodology for optimally sizing the combination of a battery bank and PV array in a wind/PV hybrid system](https://doi.org/10.1109/60.507648)** — Borowy & Salameh (1996), *IEEE Transactions on Energy Conversion*. Sizes battery and array together for a stand-alone wind/PV house supply using 30 years of hourly wind and irradiance, minimising cost at a chosen loss-of-power-supply probability. Keywords: hybrid sizing, LPSP, battery bank, stand-alone. Related: [[Energy_Transition_and_Scenarios]], [[PV_Electrical_Design_and_Inverters]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/P432BVRY)
