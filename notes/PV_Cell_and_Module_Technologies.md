## Notes

- **[A comparative life cycle assessment of silicon PV modules: Impact of module design, manufacturing location and inventory](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solmat.2021.111277)** — Müller et al. (2021), *Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells*. Compares glass-backsheet against glass-glass single-crystalline modules across manufacturing locations, filling the gap left when LCAs ignore new module designs and fast production improvement. Keywords: glass-glass modules, LCA, manufacturing location, life cycle inventory. Related: [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/BG89IVFB)
- **[Achieving Net Zero Energy Greenhouses by Integrating Semitransparent Organic Solar Cells](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joule.2019.12.018)** — Ravishankar et al. (2020), *Joule*. Energy-balance modelling with a transfer-matrix optical model showing where semitransparent organic cells, combined with low-emissivity coatings, can bring a greenhouse to net zero. Keywords: semitransparent OPV, net zero greenhouse, transfer matrix, low emissivity. Related: [[PV_Greenhouses]], [[Greenhouse_Horticulture]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/LMHSI3B4)
- **[Modeling the Electrical Production Potential of Non-Planar Photovoltaic Modules](https://doi.org/10.1109/PVSC40753.2019.8980706)** — Alpuerto & Balog (2019), *IEEE PVSC*. Conventional modelling assumes every cell on a module sees the same conditions, which flexible modules break; this models the electrical consequence of curvature. Keywords: non-planar modules, flexible PV, cell heterogeneity, MPPT. Related: [[PV_Shading_and_Mismatch]], [[PV_Electrical_Design_and_Inverters]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/VQ8QE2R6)

Keywords: champion efficiency, module records, technology progress, NREL chart. Related: [[Thin_Film_Solar_Cells]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/FKP9RINM)
