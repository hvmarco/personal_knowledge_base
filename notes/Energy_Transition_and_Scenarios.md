## Notes

- **[2015 Renewable Energy Data Book](https://www.osti.gov/scitech/biblio/1339347-renewable-energy-data-book)** — Beiter & Tian (2016), *NREL*. Annual compilation of installed capacity, generation and investment across renewable technologies — the reference numbers, gathered in one place. Keywords: capacity statistics, generation, investment, NREL data book. Related: [[Global_Datasets_and_Catalogs]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/8U5QKT6J) #type/reference
- **Green Energy Choices: The benefits, risks, and trade-offs of low-carbon technologies for electricity production** — Hertwich et al. (2016), *UNEP*. Life-cycle comparison across low-carbon generation technologies, setting out where each one's environmental burden actually falls rather than assuming any is impact-free. Keywords: low-carbon technologies, life-cycle comparison, trade-offs, UNEP. Related: [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]], [[Climate_Change]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/B4BKRANY)
- **[Wind in power — 2013 European statistics](http://www.ewea.org/fileadmin/files/library/publications/statistics/EWEA_Annual_Statistics_2013.pdf)** — EWEA (2014). Annual European wind installation and capacity statistics for 2013. Keywords: wind statistics, installed capacity, Europe, EWEA. Related: [[Global_Datasets_and_Catalogs]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/RGISBMHU) #region/europe #type/reference
- **[Windmap — The first step in wind resource assessment](http://www.windmap.se/)** — Greenbyte AB (2014). Swedish web tool for a first-pass wind resource look at a site. Keywords: wind resource, screening tool, Sweden. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/R4LKJ6B5) #region/sweden #type/tool
- **[Wind power in forests: wind and effects on loads](http://uu.diva-portal.org/smash/record.jsf?searchId=1&pid=diva2:704370)** — Bergström et al. (2013), *Elforsk*. Project V-312, run by Uppsala University with WeatherTech Scandinavia, KTH and DTU Wind Energy: how forest canopies alter the wind field and what that does to turbine loads. The core reference for Nordic forest wind siting. Keywords: wind in forests, canopy roughness, turbine loads, Elforsk V-312. Related: [[Swedish_Forestry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/EFH9Z7BN) #region/nordic
- **[Windenergie im Wald — Forstwirtschaft und Stromproduktion](http://www.juwi.de/startseite/projektentwicklung/windenergie/windenergie_im_wald.html)** (Wind energy in the forest — forestry and electricity production) — juwi Holding AG (2013). Developer's account of combining forestry with wind generation on the same land. Keywords: wind in forest, dual land use, developer perspective, Germany. Related: [[Swedish_Forestry]], [[Agrivoltaics]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/RK46XP6L) #region/europe
- **[Upwind — Design limits and solutions for very large wind turbines](http://www.upwind.eu/)** — EWEA (2011). The UpWind project's conclusions on what limits turbine scale and which solutions push the limit outward. Keywords: large turbines, design limits, UpWind, scaling. Related: [[Swedish_Forestry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/NAGEJQ5T) #region/europe
- **[Vindkraft i Dalarna: Från acceptans och lokalisering till planering och eftertanke](http://du.diva-portal.org/smash/record.jsf?pid=diva2:606375)** (Wind power in Dalarna: from acceptance and siting to planning and reflection) — Gradén (2011), PhD thesis. Examines the conditions for wind power diffusion in a Swedish county newly considered attractive for renewables — acceptance, siting and planning rather than resource. The social-licence side that solar development faces too. Keywords: acceptance, siting, planning, Dalarna, social licence. Related: [[Permitting_and_Environmental_Assessment]], [[Urban_Planning]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/9SJ36V22) #region/sweden
- **Wind Energy** — Wiser et al. (2011), *IPCC Special Report on Renewable Energy Sources and Climate Change Mitigation*. The SRREN wind chapter: resource, technology, integration, cost and deployment potential. Keywords: IPCC SRREN, wind resource, integration, deployment potential. Related: [[Climate_Change]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/QD9PKRRZ)
- **[Neues Windrad wird so hoch wie der "Maintower"](http://www.welt.de/wissenschaft/article8793775/Neues-Windrad-wird-so-hoch-wie-der-Maintower.html)** (New wind turbine will be as tall as the Maintower) — Wetzel (2010), *Welt Online*. Press piece on a 200 m turbine in the Pfalz, then the most powerful in the world. Keywords: turbine height, Germany, press. Related: [[Swedish_Forestry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/RCTTYGGA) #region/europe
- **[Understanding Renewable Energy Systems](http://www.amazon.de/dp/1844071286)** — Quaschning (2005), *Earthscan*. Textbook covering the whole renewable spectrum — solar thermal, PV, wind, hydro, biomass — with the physics and the system calculations worked through. Keywords: textbook, renewable systems, PV and wind, calculations. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]], [[Energy_Storage]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/HWUPUPGX)
