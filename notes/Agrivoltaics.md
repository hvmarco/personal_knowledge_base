# Agrivoltaics

## Notes

- **[Enabling Year-round Cultivation in the Nordics — Agrivoltaics and Adaptive LED Lighting](https://doi.org/10.20944/preprints202109.0367.v1)** — Hernandez Velasco (2021), preprint. Puts the two halves of this vault together: agrivoltaics supplying the energy and adaptive LED lighting extending the Nordic season. Keywords: agrivoltaics, adaptive LED lighting, year-round cultivation, Nordics. Related: [[Lighting_Strategy_and_Control]], [[Greenhouse_Horticulture]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/GE7RZ8ND) #region/nordic #project/own-publications
- **[Evaluation of the first agrivoltaic system in Sweden](https://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:mdh:diva-64682)** — Campana et al. (2023), report. Assessment of Sweden's first agrivoltaic installation, against a background where Swedish PV had been treated almost entirely as a building energy-efficiency measure. Keywords: Sweden, first APV system, pilot evaluation, Nordic agrivoltaics. Related: [[PV_in_Nordic_Climates]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/LQWT9D8P) #region/sweden
- **[Direct and diffuse shading factors modelling for the most representative agrivoltaic system layouts](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2023.120981)** — Zainali et al. (2023), *Applied Energy*. Models ground-level shading for fixed vertical, one-axis and two-axis layouts separately for the direct and diffuse components — the calculation that decides whether a layout costs the crop too much. Keywords: shading factors, direct and diffuse, vertical bifacial, tracking layouts. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]], [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/JG6R4SJG)
- **[Optimisation of vertically mounted agrivoltaic systems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2021.129091)** — Campana et al. (2021), *Journal of Cleaner Production*. Multi-objective optimisation of vertical bifacial APV coupling three sub-models — radiation and shading, PV, and crop yield — validated against measurements and commercial software. The methodological reference for vertical agri-PV in the Nordics. Keywords: vertical bifacial, multi-objective optimisation, crop yield model, water-food-energy nexus. Related: [[Bifacial_PV_and_Albedo]], [[Optimization_and_Decision_Making]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/E24BX442) [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/V25SCIAH) #needs-review

The agronomy-side review of agri-PV: what it asks of the cropping system rather than of the array. Keywords: agrophotovoltaics, review, challenges, agronomy. Related: [[Agriculture]]. - **[Agrivoltaic Systems Design and Assessment: A Critical Review, and a Descriptive Model towards a Sustainable Landscape Vision](https://doi.org/10.3390/su13126871)** — Toledo & Scognamiglio (2021), *Sustainability*. Argues that APV design has been driven almost entirely by efficiency and proposes a three-dimensional pattern language that treats landscape and land use as design variables in their own right. Keywords: design patterns, landscape, land use, critical review. Related: [[PV_Greenhouses]], [[Urban_Planning]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/QY8MVIRG)
- **[Combining food and energy production: Design of an agrivoltaic system applied in arable and vegetable farming in Germany](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2020.110694)** — Trommsdorff et al. (2021), *Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews*. Reports electrical yield and the response of four crops at the Fraunhofer ISE APV-RESOLA facility near Lake Constance, whose elevated design lets normal machinery pass underneath. Keywords: APV-RESOLA, elevated design, land productivity, four crops, Germany. Related: [[Agriculture]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/HNW7FZNA) #region/europe
- **[A First Investigation of Agriculture Sector Perspectives on the Opportunities and Barriers for Agrivoltaics](https://doi.org/10.3390/agronomy10121885)** — Pascaris et al. (2020), *Agronomy*. Interviews agriculture-sector experts on what would actually stop farmers adopting dual use — a reminder that the binding constraint is rarely the shading model. Keywords: adoption barriers, farmer perspectives, technology diffusion, interviews. Related: [[Agriculture]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/RX3N2672)
- **[Agrivoltaics: Opportunities for Agriculture and the Energy Transition](https://www.ise.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ise/en/documents/publications/studies/APV-Guideline.pdf)** — Trommsdorff et al. (2020), *Fraunhofer ISE*. The Fraunhofer guideline: system types, crop suitability, regulatory treatment and economics, written to be handed to a developer or an authority. Keywords: guideline, system types, regulation, Fraunhofer ISE. Related: [[Energy_Transition_and_Scenarios]], [[Permitting_and_Environmental_Assessment]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/V6ZG7U96) #type/reference
- **[Agrivoltaics provide mutual benefits across the food–energy–water nexus in drylands](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41893-019-0364-5)** — Barron-Gafford et al. (2019), *Nature Sustainability*. Measures microclimate, panel temperature, soil moisture, irrigation use and plant physiology together in a dryland APV system, and finds the benefit runs both ways — shaded crops use less water, and cooler panels perform better. Keywords: food-energy-water nexus, drylands, microclimate, panel temperature. Related: [[Agriculture]], [[Photosynthesis_and_Gas_Exchange]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/IREEP9RW)
- **[The potential of agrivoltaic systems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2015.10.024)** — Dinesh & Pearce (2016), *Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews*. Frames dual use as the answer to PV's land competition with food production, and estimates the economic value added when shade-tolerant crops are grown under an array. Keywords: land competition, shade-tolerant crops, economics, review. Related: [[PV_Greenhouses]], [[Building_Integrated_PV]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/GJ2DYCEK)
- **[Shading and electrical features of a photovoltaic array mounted inside the roof of an east–west oriented greenhouse](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biosystemseng.2010.04.007)** — Yano et al. (2010), *Biosystems Engineering*. Compares straight-line against checkerboard arrangement of 30 modules covering 12.9% of a Gothic-arch greenhouse roof, mapping how each distributes shade inside and what each generates. Keywords: array arrangement, checkerboard, greenhouse roof, shade distribution. Related: [[PV_Greenhouses]], [[Greenhouse_Horticulture]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/FYT5K7BC)
- **[On the Coexistence of Solar-Energy Conversion and Plant Cultivation](https://doi.org/10.1080/01425918208909875)** — Goetzberger & Zastrow (1982), *International Journal of Solar Energy*. The founding agrivoltaics paper: raise the collectors about 2 m and space rows at roughly three times their height, and the ground still receives about two-thirds of open-field radiation integrated over the day. Every later layout study is a refinement of this. Keywords: origin paper, row spacing, elevated collectors, ground radiation. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]], [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]].
