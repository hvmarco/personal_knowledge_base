# Solar Park Development

**Summary**: The practice of getting a utility-scale solar plant permitted, designed and built in the Nordics.
**Parent**: [[index]] · **Related**: [[Photovoltaics]], [[Geospatial]], [[Data_Sources]]

---

The working counterpart to [[Photovoltaics]]: permitting and environmental assessment, site and civil design, electrical design and grid connection. Research on phenomena that a development has to answer for — glare, EMF, noise, stormwater — lives here rather than with the physics, because it is used as evidence in an application. Modelling of PV output does not.
