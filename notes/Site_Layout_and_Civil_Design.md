## Notes

- **[Exposure site catalogue](https://efcweb.org/Scientific+Groups/WP25_+Atmospheric+Corrosion/Current+Activities/_/Exposure%20site%20catalogue%20EFC%20lores.pdf)** — European Federation of Corrosion (2021). Catalogue of atmospheric corrosion exposure sites across Europe with their classification and environment, useful for anchoring a corrosivity category to a real reference station rather than a map interpolation. Keywords: atmospheric corrosion, exposure sites, corrosivity category, ISO 9223. Related: [[PV_Degradation_and_Reliability]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/XWPG8TN2) #type/reference
- **[Corrosion protection of steel elements in façade systems – A review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jobe.2020.101759)** — Soufeiani et al. (2020), *Journal of Building Engineering*. Reviews atmospheric, galvanic, embedded and cut-edge corrosion of structural steel and what drives the rate — the same mechanisms that govern mounting structures and their fixings. Keywords: steel corrosion, galvanic corrosion, cut-edge, protection, service life. Related: [[Building_Integrated_PV]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/5TA8F6L9)
- **[Utility-Scale Solar Photovoltaic Power Plants: A Project Developer's Guide](https://www.ifc.org/wps/wcm/connect/f05d3e00498e0841bb6fbbe54d141794/IFC+Solar+Report_Web+_08+05.pdf?MOD=AJPERES)** — International Finance Corporation (2015). The IFC guide to developing, financing, constructing and operating a utility-scale plant, aimed at developers entering a market. The single most useful end-to-end reference on this page. Keywords: project development, financing, construction, IFC, utility-scale. Related: [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]], [[Permitting_and_Environmental_Assessment]]. - **[Best Practices in PV System Installation](http://www.nrel.gov/docs/fy15osti/63234.pdf)** — Doyle et al. (2015), *NREL*. Minimum installation requirements written to make solar assets legible to investors and rating agencies, and so to cut transaction costs in securitisation. Keywords: installation quality, asset transparency, securitisation, minimum requirements. Related: [[PV_Electrical_Design_and_Inverters]], [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/9XTZ5U4B) #type/reference
- **[A Tool to Optimize the Layout of Ground-Based PV Installations Taking into Account the Economic Boundary Conditions](https://doi.org/10.4229/EUPVSEC20142014-5DV.3.61)** — Mermoud & Wittmer (2014), *EU PVSEC*. Optimises ground-mount layout against the economics rather than yield alone, so row pitch and ground cover ratio are set by what the land and the losses actually cost. Keywords: layout optimisation, ground cover ratio, economic boundary conditions, row pitch. Related: [[PV_Shading_and_Mismatch]], [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/RJ9T5YV4)
- **[Mapping atmospheric corrosion in coastal regions: methods and results](https://doi.org/10.1117/1.JPE.2.022003)** — Slámová & Glaser (2012), *Journal of Photonics for Energy*. Maps coastal corrosion potential in GIS from sea-salt aerosol and chloride deposition, comparing geostatistical interpolation against a modelled distribution — directly applicable to siting near the coast. Keywords: chloride deposition, sea salt aerosol, GIS mapping, coastal siting, module degradation. Related: [[PV_Degradation_and_Reliability]], [[Geospatial_Python]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/6849DFKV)
- **[Atmospheric corrosion of metals in 2010–2039 and 2070–2099](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.atmosenv.2012.02.081)** — Tidblad (2012), *Atmospheric Environment*. Projects future corrosion of carbon steel and zinc across Europe under climate change: chloride deposition dominates in coastal and near-coastal areas, and the shift can reach a full corrosivity category. Worth knowing when specifying galvanising for a 40-year asset. Keywords: corrosion projection, carbon steel, zinc, chloride, corrosivity category. Related: [[Climate_Change]].
