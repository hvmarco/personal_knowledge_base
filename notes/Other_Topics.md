# Other Topics

**Summary**: Holding area for subjects that fit no domain yet, including [[Unsorted]].
**Parent**: [[index]] · **Related**: [[Themes]]

---

Nothing is forced into a nearest-fit page. Notes with no home are parked here under a `## Candidate: <Name>` subheading, tagged `#needs-topic`, and promoted to a real page once they reach five notes. Candidates are tracked in [[index]] and in `TAXONOMY.md`.

## Pages

- [[Unsorted]] — notes with no fitting page anywhere, grouped under candidate subheadings.
