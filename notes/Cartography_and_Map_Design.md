## Notes

- **[Vegetation in Europe](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Vegetation_Europe.png)** — Koistinen (2007), *Wikimedia Commons*. A clean public-domain vegetation-zone map of Europe — the boreal, temperate and Mediterranean bands in one figure, reusable in a report. - **[ColorBrewer: color advice for maps](https://colorbrewer2.org/#type=diverging&scheme=PuOr&n=4)** — Brewer & Harrower. The sequential, diverging and qualitative colour schemes that most mapping software ships, with colour-blind and print-safe filters. The default answer to "which colours".
