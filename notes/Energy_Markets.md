## Notes

- [Handel och prissättning (Trading and pricing) — Svenska kraftnät](https://www.svk.se/aktorsportalen/bidra-med-reserver/handel-prissattning/): SVK ancillary services. Svenska kraftnät's (Sweden's TSO) page on how reserve services are traded and priced in the Nordic balancing market. Covers six reserve products — FCR-N (frequency containment, normal), FCR-D up/down (containment, disturbance), aFRR (automatic frequency restoration), mFRR (manual frequency restoration), and FFR (fast frequency response) — and their procurement timelines: FCR and aFRR on day-ahead (D-1) auctions, mFRR capacity day-ahead with energy bids 45 minutes before the operating hour, FFR procured annually. Pricing differs by product: marginal pricing for FCR and aFRR capacity, pay-as-bid for mFRR capacity, with energy settled on Nord Pool up/down-regulation prices. Capacity payment = price x sold capacity (MW); energy payment = price x activated energy (MWh). Historical prices are published through SVK's Mimer platform and eSett, with market surveillance under the EU REMIT regulation. `ancillary-services` `frequency-reserves` `fcr-afrr-mfrr` `balancing-market` `nordic-grid` `capacity-pricing`
