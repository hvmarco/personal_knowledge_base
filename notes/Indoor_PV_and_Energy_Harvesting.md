# Indoor PV and Energy Harvesting

**Summary**: PV under artificial and low light — indoor cell characterisation, the missing standard test conditions for it, and millimetre-scale energy harvesting for sensor nodes.
**Parent**: [[Photovoltaics]] · **Related**: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]], [[PV_Shading_and_Mismatch]], [[Energy_Storage]]

---

## Notes

- **AlGaAs Photovoltaics for Indoor Energy Harvesting in mm-Scale Wireless Sensor Nodes** — Teran et al. (2015), *IEEE Transactions on Electron Devices*. Analyses the theoretical efficiency limit under typical indoor lighting and measures commercial crystalline Si against fabricated GaAs and Al₀.₂Ga₀.₈As cells — wider band gaps win indoors because the indoor spectrum is narrower and dimmer. Keywords: AlGaAs, indoor spectrum, band gap, wireless sensor nodes. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/2V3ULR2I)
- **[Energy harvesting system for wireless body sensor nodes](https://doi.org/10.1109/BioCAS.2014.6981751)** — Liberale et al. (2014), *IEEE BioCAS*. A complete thin-film-powered harvesting chain: store, acquire from a body sensor, transmit wirelessly. Keywords: body area network, thin-film harvester, storage element, sensor node. Related: [[Energy_Storage]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/4WGG3XW2)
- **[A Proposal for Typical Artificial Light Sources for the Characterization of Indoor Photovoltaic Applications](https://doi.org/10.3390/en7031500)** — Minnaert & Veelaert (2014), *Energies*. There is no international norm for characterising cells indoors and STC is irrelevant there; this simulates efficiency from cell quantum efficiency against typical artificial source spectra and proposes reference sources. The standards gap stated plainly. Keywords: indoor characterisation, artificial light spectra, quantum efficiency, missing standard. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]], [[Horticultural_Lighting]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/4ZCYLPL4) #project/msc-thesis/4-2
- **Indoor photovoltaic energy harvesting for mm-scale systems** — Teran et al. (2014). Argues ambient indoor light carries enough energy for wirelessly interconnected low-power sensor nodes, and works through what that requires of the cell. Keywords: ambient light, mm-scale systems, low-power electronics, harvesting. Related: [[Energy_Storage]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/3Z2ENN4U)
- **M3: A Mm-scale Wireless Energy Harvesting Sensor Platform** — Pannuto et al. (2013), *ACM*. The Michigan Micro Mote platform: a complete millimetre-scale wireless sensor system running on harvested energy. Keywords: M3, micro mote, sensor platform, energy harvesting. Related: [[Energy_Storage]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/3RGG8MDS)
- **[Characterization of photovoltaic modules for low-power indoor application](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2012.07.001)** — Sacco et al. (2013), *Applied Energy*. Makes the same point as Minnaert & Veelaert from the module side: indoor illumination is hard to describe adequately and no international norm regulates characterisation under it. Keywords: indoor modules, low power, illumination description, characterisation. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/X7TZU5N9)
- **[Photovoltaic Sample-and-Hold Circuit Enabling MPPT Indoors for Low-Power Systems](https://doi.org/10.1109/TCSI.2011.2173393)** — Weddell et al. (2012), *IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems I*. Ultra-low-power MPPT with a sample-and-hold and cold-start arrangement that works down to 100 lux — the first reported MPPT across genuinely indoor light levels. Keywords: MPPT, cold start, 100 lux, ultra low power. Related: [[PV_Electrical_Design_and_Inverters]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/2R5Z4MQ6) #project/msc-thesis/4-2
