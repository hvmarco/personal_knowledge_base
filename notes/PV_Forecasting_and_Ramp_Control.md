# PV Forecasting and Ramp Control

**Summary**: Deterministic and probabilistic forecasting of PV output, satellite-derived nowcasting, and ramp-rate limitation and power smoothing.
**Parent**: [[Photovoltaics]] · **Related**: [[PV_System_Performance]], [[Energy_Storage]], [[Power_Systems_and_Grid_Integration]]

---

## Notes

- **[Improved satellite-derived PV power nowcasting using real-time power data from reference PV systems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2017.10.091)** — Bright et al. (2018), *Solar Energy*. Corrects satellite-derived fleet nowcasts with live output from a handful of reference PV systems, and shows how much a single reference site already buys against satellite-only and upscaling-only baselines. Keywords: nowcasting, satellite irradiance, reference systems, fleet estimation. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/X8XNT66B)
- **[Impact of probabilistic small-scale photovoltaic generation forecast on energy management systems](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2018.02.069)** — El-Baz et al. (2018), *Solar Energy*. At household scale forecast errors are large enough to matter; feeding a probabilistic rather than deterministic PV forecast into demand-side management changes the scheduling outcome measurably. Keywords: probabilistic forecast, demand-side management, residential PV, forecast error. Related: [[Building_Integrated_PV]], [[Energy_Storage]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/7VXDUEWI)
- **[Probabilistic forecasting of electricity consumption, photovoltaic power generation and net demand of an individual building using Gaussian Processes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2017.12.104)** — van der Meer et al. (2018), *Applied Energy*. Tests covariance-function choice for GP forecasting of a single household's load, PV and net demand, and argues the usual squared-exponential kernel is a poor fit for physical processes. Keywords: Gaussian processes, covariance functions, net demand, probabilistic forecasting. Related: [[Statistics_and_Data_Analysis]], [[Building_Integrated_PV]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/JBDLRDR4)
- **[Review on probabilistic forecasting of photovoltaic power production and electricity consumption](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.rser.2017.05.212)** — van der Meer et al. (2018), *Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews*. Survey of probabilistic solar and load forecasting: since any forecast is wrong, the argument is for quantifying the error through prediction intervals rather than issuing point forecasts. Keywords: probabilistic forecasting, prediction intervals, review, load forecasting. Related: [[Statistics_and_Data_Analysis]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ZL7ZBT4Y)
- **[Control strategies to use the minimum energy storage requirement for PV power ramp-rate control](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2014.10.038)** — de la Parra et al. (2015), *Solar Energy*. Given a grid code's ramp limit, works out the control strategy that meets it with the least storage — the counterpart to Marcos et al., which sizes the store for a fixed strategy. Keywords: ramp-rate control, control strategy, minimum storage, grid codes. Related: [[Energy_Storage]], [[Power_Systems_and_Grid_Integration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/756KLULT)
- **[On the relation between battery size and PV power ramp rate limitation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2016.11.039)** — Makibar et al. (2017), *Solar Energy*. Asks what a battery actually has to be sized for: rather than absorbing every fluctuation, it trades size reduction against how often the ramp limit is then breached and what the penalty costs. Keywords: ramp-rate control, battery sizing, non-compliance penalties, fluctuations. Related: [[Energy_Storage]], [[Power_Systems_and_Grid_Integration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/QZSFNCZV)
- **[Storage requirements for PV power ramp-rate control](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2013.10.037)** — Marcos et al. (2014), *Solar Energy*. Gives a general method for the maximum power and minimum energy a storage system needs for any plant size and ramp limit, validated on a year of 5-second data from the 38.5 MW Amaraleja plant. Keywords: storage sizing, ramp-rate limit, grid codes, 5-second data. Related: [[Energy_Storage]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/V29ZRJHV)
- **[Power "smoothing" of a commercial-size photovoltaic system by an energy storage system](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICHQP.2014.6842838)** — Moumouni et al. (2014). Characterises cloud-transient fluctuations on a commercial CPV system and sizes storage for smoothing with a moving average, finding 1-second sampling necessary at that system size. Keywords: power smoothing, cloud transients, CPV, sampling interval. Related: [[Energy_Storage]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/DKW7GJLC)
- **[Comparing ramp rates from large and small PV systems, and selection of batteries for ramp rate control](https://doi.org/10.1109/PVSC.2013.6744493)** — Cormode et al. (2013). A 1.6 MW and a 2 kW system both swing more than 50% of rating in under ten seconds, and despite a 4000-fold difference in area the fluctuation bandwidth is remarkably similar — with direct consequences for battery sizing. Keywords: ramp rates, geographic smoothing, fluctuation bandwidth, battery selection. Related: [[Energy_Storage]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/7D3SUNYX)
