# Photovoltaics

**Summary**: PV technology, performance, modelling and economics — the research and physics side of solar electricity.
**Parent**: [[index]] · **Related**: [[Solar_Park_Development]], [[Agrivoltaics_and_Dual_Use]], [[Energy_Systems]]

---

Everything about how PV cells, modules and systems behave and what they cost. Modelling of irradiance and yield, measurement and standards, loss mechanisms, and the technologies themselves live here. The practice of getting a plant permitted and built is [[Solar_Park_Development]]; PV combined with crops or greenhouses is [[Agrivoltaics_and_Dual_Use]]; the software used to model any of it is [[PV_Modelling_Tools]].
