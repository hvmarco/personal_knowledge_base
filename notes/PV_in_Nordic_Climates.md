- **[Snow-Induced PV Loss Modeling Using Production-Data Inferred PV System Models](https://doi.org/10.3390/en14061574)** — van Noord et al. (2021), *Energies*. Existing snow-loss models rest on a handful of sites and seasons; this infers system models from production data instead, so losses can be estimated across many sites and climates. Reported snow losses run up to 100% monthly and 34% annually. Keywords: snow losses, production data, system inference, reanalysis. Related: [[PV_Degradation_and_Reliability]], [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/TKFSWMJP) #region/sweden
- **[Integration, Validation, and Application of a PV Snow Coverage Model in SAM](https://www.osti.gov/scitech/biblio/1374128)** — Freeman & Ryberg (2017), *NREL*. The later report on bringing the Marion snow-coverage model into SAM, with validation against measured systems. Keywords: SAM, snow coverage model, validation, NREL. Related: [[PV_Modelling_Tools]], [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ND6PXJYZ)
- **[Snöpåverkan på solelproduktion](https://energiforsk.se/program/solel/rapporter/snopaverkan-pa-solelproduktion-2017-382/)** (Snow effects on solar electricity production) — van Noord et al. (2017), *Energiforsk*. Argues that for investment decisions north of Dalarna a long-term monthly mean snow loss is enough — no need for time-resolved modelling — and that it can be applied on top of commercial simulation output. The practical Swedish answer. Keywords: snow losses, monthly means, investment decisions, Energiforsk. Related: [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/T7TUNDEM) #region/sweden

The original technical report implementing snow coverage in SAM. Keywords: SAM, snow model, implementation, NREL. Related: [[PV_Modelling_Tools]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/5U47488N) #project/msc-thesis/4-2
- **[The effects of snowfall on solar photovoltaic performance](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2013.02.014)** — Andrews et al. (2013), *Solar Energy*. A purpose-built multi-angle, multi-technology test system monitored over two winters, plus a method for extracting snow losses from 5-minute performance data correlated with meteorological observations. Keywords: snowfall losses, tilt angle, snow albedo, time-series method. Related: [[Bifacial_PV_and_Albedo]], [[PV_Degradation_and_Reliability]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/LQKFUHFU)
- **[Measured and modeled photovoltaic system energy losses from snow for Colorado and Wisconsin locations](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2013.07.029)** — Marion et al. (2013), *Solar Energy*. Two winters of measurements on six systems, roof-mounted and ground-mounted, and the resulting snow-loss model — the Marion model that SAM and pvlib implement. Keywords: Marion model, snow losses, tilt and mounting, measured and modelled. Related: [[PV_Modelling_Tools]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/GVSILHEG)
