## Notes

- **[Photovoltaic devices — Part 1: Measurement of photovoltaic current-voltage characteristics](https://webstore.iec.ch/publication/32004)** — IEC 60904-1:2020. The base I–V measurement standard for cells, modules and arrays in natural or simulated sunlight. Everything else in this page refers back to it. Keywords: IEC 60904-1, I-V measurement, procedure, cells and modules. Related: [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/SXUTQD2M) #type/regulation #project/thesis
- **Photovoltaic devices — Part 1-1: Measurement of current-voltage characteristics of multi-junction photovoltaic (PV) devices** — IEC 60904-1-1 (2017). Extends Part 1 to multi-junction devices, where each sub-cell needs its own spectral responsivity known before the measurement means anything. Keywords: IEC 60904-1-1, multi-junction, spectral responsivity, sub-cells. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/JVDTSWGN) #type/regulation #project/msc-thesis/4-1
- **[Photovoltaic energy rating data sets for Europe](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2016.03.071)** — Huld et al. (2016), *Solar Energy*. Builds climatic data sets for IEC 61853 energy rating at five European locations and tests how sensitive the rating is to each input — the practical answer to "which weather data does energy rating need". Keywords: energy rating, climatic data sets, sensitivity analysis, IEC 61853. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/YQDC7XFW) #region/europe #project/msc-thesis/4-2
- **[PV module energy rating: opportunities and limitations](https://doi.org/10.1002/pip.2618)** — Dirnberger et al. (2015), *Progress in Photovoltaics*. Computes module performance ratios for eight module types against a crystalline reference and shows what rating by predicted yield rather than STC power does and does not resolve. Keywords: energy rating, module performance ratio, STC limitations, module types. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/L8YS4S5N) #project/msc-thesis/4-2
- **Uncertainties in Energy Rating for Thin-film PV Modules** — Dirnberger (2015), PhD thesis. Traces irradiance, temperature, spectrum and angle-of-incidence uncertainty through the energy rating calculation, and asks whether measured differences between module types survive it. Keywords: uncertainty budget, thin film, energy rating, significance. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]], [[Statistics_and_Data_Analysis]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/3NNSSHYB)
- **[On the uncertainty of energetic impact on the yield of different PV technologies due to varying spectral irradiance](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2014.10.033)** — Dirnberger et al. (2015), *Solar Energy*. Quantifies the spectral effect on yield per technology, separating the device's spectral response from the pyranometer's — the two reasons broadband resource data misstates the effective irradiance. Keywords: spectral irradiance, effective irradiance, thin film, spectroradiometer. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/DDLLM5WW)
- **[IEC 61853-1: Photovoltaic Module Performance Testing and Energy Rating Assessment for Canada](http://www.sustainabletechnologies.ca/wp/wp-content/uploads/2016/01/IEC61853_FinalReport_Dec2015.pdf)** — Leidos Canada (2015), report. Applies the IEC 61853-1 matrix to Canadian conditions — the closest published analogue to a Nordic assessment. Keywords: IEC 61853-1, performance matrix, cold climate, Canada. Related: [[PV_in_Nordic_Climates]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/Y6UK7UUY) #project/msc-thesis/4-1 #project/msc-thesis/4-2
