# Forest Damage and Herbivory

**Summary**: What kills or sets back young stands — pine weevil, moose and roe deer browsing, frost and climate damage, fire — and the protection measures used against each.
**Parent**: [[Forestry]] · **Related**: [[Forest_Regeneration]], [[Forest_Seedling_Production]], [[Frost_Hardiness_and_Freezing_Tests]]

---

## Notes

- **[Forage availability and moose winter browsing in forest landscapes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.foreco.2018.03.049)** — Bergqvist et al. (2018), *Forest Ecology and Management*. Relates browsing pressure on pine to how much forage the surrounding landscape actually offers — damage is a landscape property, not a stand one. Keywords: moose browsing, forage availability, winter, landscape scale. Related: [[Swedish_Forestry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/75DKTGKC) #species/pinus-sylvestris #region/sweden
- **[Interactions between ungulates, forests, and supplementary feeding: the role of nutritional balance](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13364-016-0301-1)** — Felton et al. (2017), *Mammal Research*. Supplementary feeding is used to divert browsing, but the nutritional balance of what is fed determines whether it works or makes things worse. Keywords: supplementary feeding, ungulates, nutritional balance, browsing diversion. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/5PUFREGM) #region/sweden
- **[Effects of planting time on pine weevil damage to Norway spruce seedlings](https://doi.org/10.1080/02827581.2015.1125523)** — Wallertz et al. (2016), *Scandinavian Journal of Forest Research*. Planting date shifts how much weevil damage a seedling takes — the cheapest available control measure. Keywords: planting time, pine weevil, Hylobius abietis, damage. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/DDHMZWB9) #species/picea-abies #region/sweden
- **[To feed or not to feed? Evidence of the intended and unintended effects of feeding wild ungulates](https://doi.org/10.1002/jwmg.798)** — Milner et al. (2014), *Journal of Wildlife Management*. Reviews what supplementary feeding actually achieves against what it is intended to achieve — the unintended effects are substantial. Keywords: supplementary feeding, wild ungulates, unintended effects, review. Related: [[Swedish_Forestry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/SYTRTIT3)
- **[Skogsbranden i Västmanlands län — lärdomar för framtiden](https://www.regeringen.se/rattsliga-dokument/kommittedirektiv/2014/08/dir.-2014116/)** (The forest fire in Västmanland County — lessons for the future) — Swedish Government (2014). The inquiry terms of reference after Sweden's largest modern forest fire. Keywords: forest fire, Västmanland, inquiry, lessons. Related: [[Climate_Change]], [[Swedish_Forestry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/DWUVL768) #region/sweden
- **[Effects of timing, duration, and intensity of simulated browsing on Scots pine growth and stem quality](https://doi.org/10.1080/02827581.2014.960896)** — Wallgren et al. (2014), *Scandinavian Journal of Forest Research*. Separates when, how long and how hard browsing occurs, and shows the three affect stem quality differently. Keywords: simulated browsing, timing, intensity, stem quality. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/37ENEG8W) #species/pinus-sylvestris #region/sweden
- **[Effects of roe deer browsing and site preparation on performance of planted broadleaved and conifer seedlings](https://doi.org/10.1080/02827580903117420)** — Bergquist et al. (2009), *Scandinavian Journal of Forest Research*. Crosses browsing pressure with site preparation — the two interact, and preparation can partly offset browsing. Keywords: roe deer browsing, site preparation, broadleaves, conifers. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/K2PH37C3) #species/picea-abies #project/course-reading
- **Mini-seedlings of *Picea abies* are less attacked by *Hylobius abietis* than conventional ones** — Pettersson et al. (2008), *Scandinavian Journal of Forest Research*. A direct argument for the miniplant system: smaller stock takes less weevil damage, which offsets its other disadvantages. Keywords: mini-seedlings, pine weevil, attack rate, stock type. Related: [[Forest_Seedling_Production]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/IRX82G46) #species/picea-abies
- **[Quantitative estimates of tree species selectivity by moose in a forest landscape](https://doi.org/10.1080/02827580701515023)** — Månsson et al. (2007), *Scandinavian Journal of Forest Research*. Quantifies which species moose actually prefer, which is what determines where damage concentrates after a species-choice decision. Keywords: species selectivity, moose, browsing preference, landscape. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/UUGAPC8Y) #species/picea-abies #project/course-reading
- **[Soil features affecting damage to conifer seedlings by the pine weevil *Hylobius abietis*](https://doi.org/10.1093/forestry/cpi008)** — Petersson et al. (2005), *Forestry*. Ties weevil damage to the soil conditions at the planting spot — which is why scarification works as a control measure. Keywords: soil features, pine weevil, planting spot, scarification. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/XB84RZD9) #species/picea-abies #project/course-reading
- **[General Biology and Life Cycles of Bark Weevils](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4020-2241-8_14)** — Day et al. (2004), *Bark and Wood Boring Insects in Living Trees in Europe*. The biology chapter: what the weevil's life cycle is, and therefore when and where the seedling is exposed. Keywords: bark weevils, life cycle, biology, Europe. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/5JXWEGET)

(2001), *Forest Ecology and Management*. Site preparation changes the near-ground microclimate and so the frost risk; seedling type changes how much of it matters. Keywords: frost damage, site preparation, seedling type, microclimate. Related: [[Frost_Hardiness_and_Freezing_Tests]], [[Forest_Regeneration]]. Keywords: reforestation methods, pine weevil, seedling survival, comparison. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]].
