## Notes

- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): An open global dataset of 2.6 million historical flood events derived from news articles spanning more than 150 countries, published on Zenodo (v1, 2026-02-15) as a single 667 MB Parquet file under CC BY 4.0. Authored by 15 researchers from Google, Bar-Ilan University, UC Davis, and the University of Alabama, including Rotem Mayo, Frederik Kratzert, Grey Nearing, Yossi Matias, and Deborah Cohen. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18647054. `flood-events` `disaster-monitoring` `open-dataset` `news-extraction` `parquet` `hydrology`
