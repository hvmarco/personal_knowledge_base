## Notes

- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054) — open global dataset of 2.6 million historical flood events extracted from news articles across more than 150 countries, published on Zenodo (v1, 2026-02-15) as a single 667 MB Parquet file under CC BY 4.0. Fifteen authors from Google, Bar-Ilan University, UC Davis and the University of Alabama, including Mayo, Kratzert, Nearing, Matias and Cohen. DOI 10.5281/zenodo.18647054. Keywords: flood events, disaster monitoring, news extraction, parquet, hydrology. Related: [[Data]], [[Machine_Learning]], [[Remote_Sensing]] #type/dataset #region/global
- **[Trends in evergreen oak suitability from assembled species distribution models: assessing climate change in south-western Europe](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11056-018-9629-5)** — López-Tirado et al. (2018), *New Forests*. Ensemble species-distribution modelling for four evergreen oaks under climate scenarios — where the Iberian oak forest can still be expected to sit. Keywords: species distribution models, evergreen oak, climate scenarios, Iberia. Related: [[Forest_Regeneration]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/K4C7F3SC) #region/europe
- **[Primary forest cover loss in Indonesia over 2000-2012](https://doi.org/10.1038/nclimate2277)** — Margono et al. (2014), *Nature Climate Change*. Landsat-based, spatially and temporally explicit accounting of Indonesian primary forest clearing — the study that settled the disputed numbers. Keywords: deforestation, primary forest, Indonesia, Landsat. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]], [[Data]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/K6FD625T)
- **[Agriculture, forestry and other land use (AFOLU)](http://www.forskningsdatabasen.dk/en/catalog/2261004406)** — Smith et al. (2014), *IPCC AR5 Working Group III*. The AFOLU mitigation chapter: what land-sector abatement is available and at what cost. Keywords: AFOLU, IPCC AR5, mitigation, land use. Related: [[Forestry]], [[Agriculture]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/LQ3XJGUS) #type/reference
- **[Agriculture, forestry and other land use emissions by sources and removals by sinks](http://www.fao.org/docrep/019/i3671e/i3671e.pdf)** — Tubiello et al. (2014), *FAO Statistics Division*. Documents the FAOSTAT emissions database — global AFOLU emissions and removals, with the method behind them. Keywords: FAOSTAT, emissions database, AFOLU, sinks. Related: [[Forestry_and_Agriculture_Statistics]], [[Global_Datasets_and_Catalogs]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/NNSLYY7B) #type/dataset
- **Ontario's forests and forestry in a changing climate** — Colombo (2008), *Ontario Forest Research Institute*. Review of what elevated CO2 and a warming climate do to forest plants and communities, written for a forest agency rather than a journal. Keywords: climate change, forest response, elevated CO2, Ontario. Related: [[Forest_Seedling_Production]], [[Forest_Regeneration]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/2JH34W7M) #type/reference
- **[Global land environments since the last interglacial](http://www.esd.ornl.gov/ern/qen/nerc.html)** — Adams & Faure (1997), *Oak Ridge National Laboratory*. The palaeo-vegetation map series for the last glacial cycle, kept online as a reference — useful for the baseline question of what grew where before. Keywords: palaeovegetation, last interglacial, ecosystem maps, glacial cycle. Related: [[Global_Datasets_and_Catalogs]], [[Swedish_Forestry]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/43DMK9F2) #type/dataset
