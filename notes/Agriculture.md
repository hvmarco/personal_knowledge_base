## Notes

- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound) — Python package for delineating agricultural field boundaries, by Sayak Majumdar (Desert Research Institute). Combines seven methods — YOLO-based Delineate-Anything detection, Fields of The World semantic segmentation, DINOv3 segmentation, Prithvi-EO-2.0 inference, unsupervised embedding clustering, supervised fine-tuning, and multi-engine ensembling — pulling from 10 sources (Sentinel-2, Landsat, HLS, NAIP, SPOT 6/7, Google Satellite Embeddings, TESSERA) through Earth Engine, refining with SAM2 and writing fiboa-compliant GeoPackage/GeoJSON/GeoParquet. Python 3.10+, Apache 2.0, manuscript in preparation for *Remote Sensing of Environment* (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.19229665). Keywords: field boundaries, crop mapping, segmentation, Google Earth Engine, fiboa. Related: [[Deep_Learning]], [[Embeddings]], [[Python]], [[Remote_Sensing]] #type/tool
- **[Managing agricultural drought in Sweden using a novel spatially-explicit model from the perspective of water-food-energy nexus](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclepro.2018.06.096)** — Campana et al. (2018), *Journal of Cleaner Production*. Couples crop water demand, water availability, yield and the electricity irrigation would need, spatially, for Swedish conditions. The paper that makes irrigation an energy question here. Keywords: drought, irrigation, water-food-energy nexus, spatial model. Related: [[Climate_Change]], [[Energy_Systems]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/TFQKAJEH) #region/sweden
- **[Irrigation water requirement and water withdrawal by country](http://www.fao.org/3/a-bc824e.pdf)** — Frenken & Gillet (2012), *FAO*. The country-level irrigation requirement and withdrawal figures behind AQUASTAT. Keywords: irrigation requirement, water withdrawal, FAO, country statistics. Related: [[Global_Datasets_and_Catalogs]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/LXQBEWCN) #type/dataset
- **[The water footprint of humanity](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1109936109)** — Hoekstra & Mekonnen (2012), *PNAS*. Maps green, blue and grey water footprints at high resolution, from both the production and the consumption side — the source of most water-footprint numbers in circulation. Keywords: water footprint, green water, blue water, virtual water trade. Related: [[Vertical_Farming_and_Urban_Agriculture]], [[Climate_Change]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/EVIFZNAZ)
- **[Monitoring agricultural water use at country level: experiences of a pilot project in Benin and Ethiopia](http://agris.fao.org/agris-search/search.do?recordID=XF2006449659)** — Frenken & Kiersch (2011), *FAO*. What it takes to actually measure agricultural water use where no metering exists. Keywords: water use monitoring, Benin, Ethiopia, FAO pilot. Related: [[Global_Datasets_and_Catalogs]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/UB2T4CVS)
