## Notes

- **[rnaturalearth: World Map Data from Natural Earth](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rnaturalearth)** — South (2017), *CRAN*. Natural Earth vectors packaged for R, so country and coastline layers come from a function call rather than a download. Keywords: rnaturalearth, base map, vector data, R package. Related: [[R]], [[Cartography_and_Map_Design]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/JY5K2EJM) #type/tool
- **[AQUASTAT — FAO's global water information system](http://www.fao.org/nr/water/aquastat/water_use/index.stm)** — FAO (2016). The reference source for national water withdrawal and agricultural water use, and the basis of most country-level water comparisons. Keywords: AQUASTAT, water withdrawal, irrigation, FAO. Related: [[Agriculture]], [[Climate_Change]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/NE2KRF5T) #type/portal #region/global
- **[World Population Prospects: the 2015 revision, key findings and advance tables](http://esa.un.org/unpd/wpp/Publications/Files/Key_Findings_WPP_2015.pdf)** — United Nations (2015). The UN population projections — the denominator under every per-capita food, water and energy argument in this vault. Keywords: population projections, UN WPP, demography, key findings. Related: [[Vertical_Farming_and_Urban_Agriculture]], [[Climate_Change]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/7CD4EQ8S) #type/dataset #region/global
- **[Natural Earth — free vector and raster map data](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)** — Natural Earth. Public-domain base cartography at three scales, built to be used rather than merely published: coastlines, borders, populated places, shaded relief. Keywords: Natural Earth, base map, public domain, vector and raster. Related: [[Cartography_and_Map_Design]], [[Geospatial]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/NEGKZD2Q) #type/dataset #region/global
