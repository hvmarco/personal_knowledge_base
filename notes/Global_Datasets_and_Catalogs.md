## Notes

- [Home | OpenTopography](https://opentopography.org/) — Portal for global lidar, DEM and point-cloud data, with free registration and API access. Keywords: OpenTopography, lidar, DEM, point clouds. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]]. #region/global #type/dataset
- [awesome-gee-community-catalog](https://gee-community-catalog.org/) — Community-maintained catalog of datasets ingested into Earth Engine outside Google's own catalog — check here before assuming a dataset needs manual upload. Keywords: GEE community catalog, Earth Engine, dataset index, awesome-list. Related: [[Google_Earth_Engine]]. #type/dataset
- [Mapping Building Density with Open Building Datasets – Spatial Thoughts](https://spatialthoughts.com/2023/09/16/building-density-gee/) — Combines open building-footprint datasets in Earth Engine to map building density. Keywords: building density, open buildings, Earth Engine, Spatial Thoughts. Related: [[Google_Earth_Engine]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. #type/dataset
- [Exploring the Global 30m Land Cover Change Dataset (1985-2022) GLC_FCS30D – Spatial Thoughts](https://spatialthoughts.com/2024/06/29/global-landcover-glcfcs30d/) — Walkthrough of the GLC_FCS30D 30 m global land-cover change dataset in Earth Engine — annual land-cover classes at a resolution usable for site-scale change detection. Keywords: GLC_FCS30D, land cover, change detection, 30m resolution. Related: [[Google_Earth_Engine]], [[Remote_Sensing]]. #type/dataset
- [Tracker Map - Global Energy Monitor](https://globalenergymonitor.org/projects/global-solar-power-tracker/tracker-map/) — Global Energy Monitor's interactive map of tracked power projects worldwide, including solar — status, capacity and ownership per project. Keywords: Global Energy Monitor, project tracker, solar map, global. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]]. #region/global #type/dataset
- [Wiki-Solar mapping of utility-scale solar projects for a specified continent or sub-continent](https://www.wiki-solar.org/map/) — Wiki-Solar's map of utility-scale solar projects by continent — a precedent and competitor-landscape reference. Keywords: Wiki-Solar, utility-scale, project map, continent. Related: [[Remote_Sensing]]. #region/global #type/dataset
- [PVMAPS](https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/pvgis-photovoltaic-geographical-information-system/pvgis-data-download/pvmaps_en) — JRC's distribution page for the PVMAPS dataset and toolkit, companion to the PVMAPS references already on [[PV_Modelling_Tools]]. Keywords: PVMAPS, JRC, download, regional PV data. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. #region/europe #type/api
- **[rnaturalearth: World Map Data from Natural Earth](https://CRAN.R-project.org/package=rnaturalearth)** — South (2017), *CRAN*. Natural Earth vectors packaged for R, so country and coastline layers come from a function call rather than a download. Keywords: rnaturalearth, base map, vector data, R package. Related: [[R]], [[Cartography_and_Map_Design]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/JY5K2EJM) #type/tool
- **[AQUASTAT — FAO's global water information system](http://www.fao.org/nr/water/aquastat/water_use/index.stm)** — FAO (2016). The reference source for national water withdrawal and agricultural water use, and the basis of most country-level water comparisons. Keywords: AQUASTAT, water withdrawal, irrigation, FAO. Related: [[Agriculture]], [[Climate_Change]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/NE2KRF5T) #type/portal #region/global
- **[World Population Prospects: the 2015 revision, key findings and advance tables](http://esa.un.org/unpd/wpp/Publications/Files/Key_Findings_WPP_2015.pdf)** — United Nations (2015). The UN population projections — the denominator under every per-capita food, water and energy argument in this vault. Keywords: population projections, UN WPP, demography, key findings. Related: [[Vertical_Farming_and_Urban_Agriculture]], [[Climate_Change]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/7CD4EQ8S) #type/dataset #region/global
- **[Natural Earth — free vector and raster map data](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)** — Natural Earth. Public-domain base cartography at three scales, built to be used rather than merely published: coastlines, borders, populated places, shaded relief. Keywords: Natural Earth, base map, public domain, vector and raster. Related: [[Cartography_and_Map_Design]], [[Geospatial]].
