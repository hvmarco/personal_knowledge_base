# PV Shading and Mismatch

**Summary**: Partial shading and mismatch losses — bypass diodes, array and string configurations, and behaviour under weak and low light.

## Notes

- **[Irradiance modelling for individual cells of shaded solar photovoltaic arrays](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2014.09.037)** — Goss et al. (2014), *Solar Energy*. Design software had reached the string or module as its smallest unit, which cannot express cell-level mismatch; this generates a per-timestep irradiance map down to individual cells or cell portions, accounting for near and far obstructions. Keywords: cell-level irradiance, shading algorithm, mismatch, irradiance map. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]], [[PV_Modelling_Tools]]. (2013), *EU PVSEC*. The ground-occupation-ratio versus shading-loss trade-off in free-field plants, compared across array configurations. Keywords: ground occupation ratio, array configuration, free-field plants, shading losses. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. - **[Performance Evaluation of Different PV-Array Configurations under Weak Light Conditions and Partial Shadings](http://du.diva-portal.org/smash/record.jsf?aq2=%5B%5B%5D%5D&c=2&af=%5B%5D&searchType=SIMPLE&query=&language=en&pid=diva2%3A764930&aq=%5B%5B%7B%22personId%22%3A%22mhv+OR+0000-0001-6805-2112%22%7D%5D%5D&sf=all&aqe=%5B%5D&sortOrder=author_sort_asc&onlyFullText=false&noOfRows=50&dswid=-8216)** — Hernandez Velasco (2012), MSc thesis. Empirical analysis of five commercial German PV plants, covering both the weak-light behaviour of the different module types and the effect of array configuration under partial shading. Keywords: weak light, array configuration, field data, Germany. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/YDHSFSET) #project/own-publications #project/zephyr
- **[Optimization of Row-Arrangement in PV Systems, Shading Loss Evaluations According to Module Positioning and Connexions](https://doi.org/10.4229/27thEUPVSEC2012-5AV.1.46)** — Mermoud (2012), *EU PVSEC*. Separates the year-long shading loss into beam, diffuse, albedo and electrical-mismatch components for row arrangements, and identifies tilt and shading limit angle as the parameters that matter for yield versus ground cover ratio. Keywords: row arrangement, shading limit angle, GCR, loss components. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]], [[Bifacial_PV_and_Albedo]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/R3FINTEU)
- **[Self-shading losses of fixed free-standing PV arrays](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.renene.2011.03.011)** — Brecl & Topič (2011), *Renewable Energy*. Shows that estimating self-shading loss from irradiation loss alone overestimates it, and builds a simulation model of the real energy loss as a function of row distance. Keywords: self-shading, row distance, energy loss, simulation model. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]], [[PV_System_Performance]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/Y8SMAWYG)
- **[Modeling and simulation of photovoltaic (PV) system during partial shading based on a two-diode model](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.simpat.2011.04.005)** — Ishaque et al. (2011), *Simulation Modelling Practice and Theory*. Uses the two-diode cell model, which needs only four parameters and holds up better at low irradiance, to predict array behaviour under partial shading and to drive MPPT algorithm testing. Keywords: two-diode model, partial shading, low irradiance, MPPT. Related: [[PV_Electrical_Design_and_Inverters]], [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/X65YZP9Y)
- **[An investigation on partial shading of PV modules with different connection configurations of PV cells](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.energy.2011.02.052)** — Wang & Hsu (2011), *Energy*. Compares simple-series, series-parallel, total-cross-tied, bridge-linked and honeycomb cell interconnection under shading, analysing each network with the cells' nonlinearity retained. Keywords: TCT, bridge-linked, honeycomb, interconnection topology. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/X8TP4XI6)
- **[Experimental model to estimate shading losses on PV arrays](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solmat.2010.07.029)** — Martínez-Moreno et al. (2010), *Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells*. A power-level model needing only the shaded area fraction and the number of bypass-diode blocks affected, so it drops straight into ordinary yield software without I–V curve simulation. Keywords: shading loss model, bypass blocks, shaded fraction, yield software. Related: [[PV_Modelling_Tools]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/UXG5XGSG)
- **[Partial shadings on PV arrays: by-pass diode benefits analysis](http://www.cuepe.ch/html/biblio/pdf/Shadings_Article_Valence2010.pdf)** — Mermoud & Lejeune (2010). Quantifies what bypass diodes actually recover under realistic partial shading, and where they do not help. Keywords: bypass diodes, partial shading, recovery, array behaviour. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/EVN8TDRM)
- **Schwachlichtverhalten von PV Modulen und die Erfordernis besserer Datenqualität** (Low-light behaviour of PV modules and the need for better data quality) — Schumann (2010). Argues for extended mandatory test conditions: datasheets give only STC values, yet annual irradiance distributions mean low-light response materially changes simulated yield. Keywords: low-light response, datasheet limitations, test conditions, data quality. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]], [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. - **[Development of a suitable model for characterizing photovoltaic arrays with shaded solar cells](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solener.2006.12.001)** — Karatepe et al. (2007), *Solar Energy*. Builds a module model that carries bypass diodes and condition-dependent equivalent-circuit parameters, in order to find which interconnection configuration is least susceptible to mismatch. Keywords: array model, bypass diodes, non-uniform irradiation, interconnection. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/9P88X4NP)
- **[Experimental study of mismatch and shading effects in the I–V characteristic of a photovoltaic module](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solmat.2005.04.022)** — Alonso-García et al. (2006), *Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells*. A module built with per-cell access, measured in forward and reverse bias, documenting cell parameter scatter and showing how the shaded cell's reverse characteristic governs module output. Keywords: reverse bias, cell scatter, shading profiles, I-V characteristic. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/9NPR75DV) #project/msc-thesis/4-2

(2006), *TÜV Immissionsschutz und Energiesysteme*. Compares indoor simulator characterisation against long-term natural-light measurement for the same modules. - **[Dependence of diffuse light blocking on the ground cover ratio for stationary PV arrays](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0038-092X(97)00083-2)** — van Schalkwijk et al. (1997), *Solar Energy*. Three 10 kWp flat-roof systems in Portugal and the Netherlands: inter-array shading costs up to 14% at GCR 0.61 and 30° tilt when the diffuse fraction is high, and a rear façade adds about 5%. The diffuse-blocking case that pure beam shading analysis misses. Keywords: ground cover ratio, diffuse blocking, flat roof, inter-array shading. Related: [[Site_Layout_and_Civil_Design]], [[Building_Integrated_PV]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/VRQ3MCUY) #region/netherlands
- **[Simulation der Abschattungsverluste bei solarelektrischen Systemen](http://www.volker-quaschning.de/downloads/abschattungsverluste.pdf)** (Simulation of shading losses in solar electric systems) — Quaschning (1996), *Köster*. The book-length treatment: shading is never fully avoidable — droppings, leaves, snow, surroundings — and was the main cause of underperformance in the German 1000-Roofs Programme, where more than half the generators were partially shaded. Keywords: shading simulation, 1000-Roofs Programme, horizon, loss modelling. Related: [[PV_Modelling_Tools]], [[PV_System_Performance]].
