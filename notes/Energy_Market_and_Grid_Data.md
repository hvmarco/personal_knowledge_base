## Notes

- [Transparency Platform](https://transparency.entsoe.eu/) — ENTSO-E's Transparency Platform: generation, load, cross-border flow and price data for the whole European grid, with a public API. Keywords: ENTSO-E, transparency platform, generation data, API. Related: [[Electricity_Markets_and_Prices]]. #type/api #region/europe
- [API Instructions — Fingrid Open Data](https://data.fingrid.fi/en/instructions) — how to query Fingrid's open grid-data API: authentication, endpoints and rate limits for Finnish transmission-system data. Keywords: Fingrid, open data, API, Finland. Related: [[Power_Systems_and_Grid_Integration]]. #type/api #region/finland
- [FAQ — Nord Pool Developer Portal](https://developers.nordpoolgroup.com/reference/data-faq) — getting-started FAQ for the Nord Pool day-ahead market data API: access, data coverage and common questions. Keywords: Nord Pool, API, day-ahead data, FAQ. Related: [[Electricity_Markets_and_Prices]]. #type/api #region/nordic
- **[Historical Market Data](http://www.nordpoolspot.com/historical-market-data/)** — Nord Pool AS (2017). Day-ahead and intraday price, volume and capacity series for the Nordic and Baltic bidding zones, downloadable by year. The starting point for any Nordic price analysis. Keywords: Nord Pool, day-ahead prices, bidding zones, historical series. Related: [[Electricity_Markets_and_Prices]], [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]].
