## Notes

- **[Historical Market Data](http://www.nordpoolspot.com/historical-market-data/)** — Nord Pool AS (2017). Day-ahead and intraday price, volume and capacity series for the Nordic and Baltic bidding zones, downloadable by year. The starting point for any Nordic price analysis. Keywords: Nord Pool, day-ahead prices, bidding zones, historical series. Related: [[Electricity_Markets_and_Prices]], [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]]. - **[International Fuel Prices 2014](https://www.giz.de/fachexpertise/downloads/giz-2015-en-ifp2014(1).pdf)** — Wagner (2015), *GIZ*. Retail petrol and diesel prices for some 170 countries with the tax and subsidy component separated out — the standard reference for cross-country fuel price comparison.
