Builds an uncertainty budget for a hypothetical 10 MW plant in Toronto: roughly 3.9% from year-to-year climate variability, 5% from the long-term horizontal irradiance estimate, plus model and degradation terms. The template for a P50/P90 statement. Keywords: yield uncertainty, P90, climate variability, uncertainty budget. Related: [[PV_System_Performance]], [[Statistics_and_Data_Analysis]].
