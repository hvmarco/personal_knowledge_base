# Plant Photobiology

**Summary**: Light and plants — spectrum, intensity and duration, the photoreceptors that read them, and the photosynthesis and physiology that follow.
**Parent**: [[index]] · **Related**: [[Controlled_Environment_Agriculture]], [[Forestry]], [[Agrivoltaics_and_Dual_Use]]

---

The doctoral core of this vault: how much light, of what spectrum, over what photoperiod, and what the plant does about it. Lighting hardware and strategy sit on [[Horticultural_Lighting]], the photoreceptor and morphology side on [[Light_Quality_and_Photomorphogenesis]], and gas exchange and fluorescence on [[Photosynthesis_and_Gas_Exchange]] and [[Chlorophyll_Fluorescence]]. Applications in nurseries are under [[Forestry]]; in greenhouses under [[Controlled_Environment_Agriculture]].
