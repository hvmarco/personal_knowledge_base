# Radiometry and Photometry

**Summary**: The measurement foundation under all the light work: SI and photometric units, CIE and IUPAC definitions, quanta versus energy, and the classic papers the definitions rest on.
**Parent**: [[Plant_Photobiology]] · **Related**: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]], [[Light_Quality_and_Photomorphogenesis]], [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]

## Notes

- **[The International System of Units (SI)](http://www.bipm.org/en/si/si_brochure/)** — Bureau International des Poids et Mesures (2019), *BIPM*. The SI brochure itself: base and derived units, and the 2019 redefinition tying the kilogram, ampere, kelvin and mole to fixed constants. The reference for getting radiometric and photometric units right. Keywords: SI units, base units, definitions, metrology. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/2TWX24XW) #project/thesis
- **[Quantification of UV radiation](http://www.cabi.org/cabebooks/ebook/20173304600)** — Aphalo (2017), *UV-B radiation and plant life: molecular biology to ecology*. Why UV-B is awkward to quantify — sensor bandwidth, weighting functions, the mismatch between action spectra and instrument response — and how to get defensible irradiance and dose estimates anyway. Keywords: UV-B, action spectra, weighting functions, dosimetry. Related: [[Light_Quality_and_Photomorphogenesis]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/ZKCX6XYT) #project/thesis
- **[Know Your Standard: Clarifying the CIE Erythema Action Spectrum](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1751-1097.2010.00871.x)** — Webb et al. (2011), *Photochemistry and Photobiology*. Several versions of the erythema action spectrum circulate but only one is the endorsed standard, and the choice shifts erythemally weighted irradiance by a few percent. Matters for anyone comparing UV radiometers or citing SED/MED. Keywords: erythema action spectrum, UV index, standard erythema dose, radiometer calibration. Related: [[Light_Quality_and_Photomorphogenesis]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/4IZEBMJ6)
- **IES approved method for the electrical and photometric measurements of solid-state lighting products** — IESNA Testing Procedures Committee et al. (2008), *Illuminating Engineering Society*. LM-79, the reference procedure for measuring output, efficacy and colour of LED products in integrating spheres and goniophotometers. The document behind most fixture datasheets. Keywords: LM-79, goniophotometry, integrating sphere, luminous efficacy, SSL testing. Related: [[Horticultural_Lighting]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/F4MQ3L3M)
- **[Glossary of terms used in photochemistry, 3rd edition (IUPAC Recommendations 2006)](https://doi.org/10.1351/pac200779030293)** — Braslavsky (2007), *Pure and Applied Chemistry*. The IUPAC vocabulary for photochemistry, with the radiation-related units reconciled against internationally agreed terms. Useful as the arbiter when photobiology and radiometry literature use the same word differently. Keywords: photochemistry, terminology, radiation units, IUPAC. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/8PDYJCZW)
- **Modelling of surface global radiation and CIE-weighted UV-radiation for the period 1980-2000** — Jansson & Josefsson (2007), *SMHI*. Swedish reconstruction of surface global radiation and CIE-weighted UV over two decades, giving a national long-term reference series. Keywords: global radiation, CIE-weighted UV, Sweden, reconstruction. Related: [[Solar_and_Weather_Data]], [[Light_Quality_and_Photomorphogenesis]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/MFS6UI2W) #region/sweden
- **[A luminous efficiency function, V*(λ), for daylight adaptation](https://doi.org/10.1167/5.11.3)** — Sharpe et al. (2005), *Journal of Vision*. A replacement for the CIE 1924 V(λ) built from flicker photometry on 40 genotyped observers and consistent with the Stockman & Sharpe cone fundamentals, correcting the known short-wavelength error. Keywords: luminous efficiency, V(lambda), cone fundamentals, flicker photometry. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]], [[Horticultural_Lighting]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/W3CXW2V2)
- **[IEEE Standard Letter Symbols for Units of Measurement](https://doi.org/10.1109/IEEESTD.2004.94618)** — IEEE (2004), *IEEE Std 260.1-2004*. Letter symbols for SI, inch-pound and other units — symbols only, not abbreviations for technical terms or physical quantities. Keywords: unit symbols, notation, IEEE 260.1. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/GGQXDHHH) #project/msc-thesis/4-1
- **ISO 15469:2004 — Spatial distribution of daylight, CIE standard general sky** — International Organization for Standardization (2004). Standardises fifteen sky luminance distributions spanning overcast to cloudless, as the reference sky set for daylight calculation. Keywords: CIE standard general sky, luminance distribution, daylight, ISO 15469. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]], [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/F5PB6CBD) #project/light-model
- **CIE general sky standard defining luminance distributions** — Darula & Kittler (2002). Sets out the calculation procedure and parametrisation behind the fifteen standard skies, generalising the earlier CIE clear-sky formula into one continuous family. The companion paper to ISO 15469. Keywords: sky luminance, sky parametrisation, daylight modelling, CIE. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]], [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/HESE3AMV)
- **Photometric, radiometric, and quantum light units of measure: a review of procedures for interconversion** — Thimijan & Heins (1983), *HortScience*. The conversion tables horticulture still runs on: lux to W m⁻² to µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, worked per lamp type because the factor depends on source spectrum. Keywords: unit conversion, lux, PPFD, quantum units, lamp spectra. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]], [[Horticultural_Lighting]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/NYUBDS2T) #project/light-model
- **Commission internationale de l'éclairage proceedings, 1931** — CIE (1932), *Cambridge University Press*. The proceedings that fixed the 1931 standard observer and the XYZ colorimetric system — the origin point for photometric units. Keywords: CIE 1931, standard observer, colorimetry. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/PH4BNR2E) #project/msc-thesis
- **[The C.I.E. colorimetric standards and their use](https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-4878/33/3/301)** — Smith & Guild (1931), *Transactions of the Optical Society*. States and explains the new international standards — standard observer, three illuminants, viewing conditions, trichromatic system — with the tables that made them usable. Keywords: colorimetry, standard observer, illuminants, trichromatic coordinates. Related: [[PV_Standards_and_Measurement]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/3X8ZFLRH) #project/msc-thesis
- **[Einstein's law of photochemical equivalence. Introductory address to Part I](https://doi.org/10.1039/TF9262100438)** — Allmand (1926), *Transactions of the Faraday Society*. Early assessment of the one-photon-one-molecule law and where measured quantum yields depart from it — the conceptual root of counting photons rather than energy. Keywords: photochemical equivalence, quantum yield, photochemistry history. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/LPZRRRFG)
- **[Über einen die Erzeugung und Verwandlung des Lichtes betreffenden heuristischen Gesichtspunkt](http://sedici.unlp.edu.ar/handle/10915/2784)** (On a heuristic point of view concerning the production and transformation of light) — Einstein (1905), *Annalen der Physik*. The light-quantum paper: radiation in the Wien regime behaves thermodynamically as independent energy quanta, which explains the photoelectric effect. Why photon flux is the physically meaningful quantity for photobiology. Keywords: light quanta, photoelectric effect, photon, 1905. Related: [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/NBPNGAVB)
- **[Ueber das Gesetz der Energieverteilung im Normalspectrum](https://doi.org/10.1002/andp.19013090310)** (On the law of energy distribution in the normal spectrum) — Planck (1901), *Annalen der Physik*. Derives the blackbody spectral distribution by quantising oscillator energy, introducing *h* and with it the energy-per-photon relation every quantum-to-energy conversion uses. Keywords: blackbody radiation, Planck's law, quantisation, h. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]], [[PAR_and_Spectral_Radiation]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/LYNWMZ7F)
