# Thin Film Solar Cells

- **[Alternative back contacts for CZTS thin film solar cells](http://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:uu:diva-403583)** — Englund (2020), PhD thesis, Uppsala University. Investigates back contacts beyond molybdenum for Cu₂ZnSnS₄, for both single-junction and tandem configurations — the interface that limits CZTS device performance. Keywords: CZTS, back contact, tandem, interface. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/PVBCU6YF) #region/sweden
- **[Sputtering-based processes for thin film chalcogenide solar cells on steel substrates](http://urn.kb.se/resolve?urn=urn:nbn:se:uu:diva-329778)** — Bras (2017), PhD thesis, Uppsala University. Sputtering routes for chalcogenide cells on steel, aiming at the flexible, roll-to-roll form factor that CIGS promises but glass substrates prevent. Keywords: sputtering, chalcogenide, steel substrate, flexible modules. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/AYLU9EDG) #region/sweden
- **[Substitution of the CdS buffer layer in CIGS thin-film solar cells](https://doi.org/10.1002/vipr.201400546)** — Witte et al. (2014), *Vakuum in Forschung und Praxis*. Survey of cadmium-free buffer alternatives — Zn(O,S), In₂S₃, (Zn,Sn)Oy, (Zn,Mg)O — and the deposition routes that make each work. The route out of cadmium in CIGS. Keywords: CdS buffer, cadmium-free, Zn(O,S), chemical bath deposition. Related: [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/6UKWPFTT)
- **[The Effect of Sputtering Parameters on the Film Properties of Molybdenum Back Contact for CIGS Solar Cells](https://doi.org/10.1155/2013/390824)** — Huang et al. (2013), *International Journal of Photoenergy*. Uses a Taguchi design to find the DC magnetron sputtering settings that give the best Mo film on soda-lime glass. Keywords: molybdenum, sputtering parameters, Taguchi method, soda-lime glass. Related: [[Statistics_and_Data_Analysis]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/3ZZRQEE4)
- **Fabrication of CuInTe₂ and CuInTe₂₋ₓSeₓ Ternary Gradient Quantum Dots and Their Application to Solar Cells** — Kim et al. (2013), *ACS Nano*. First colloidal synthesis of gradient-alloyed CuInTe₂ quantum dots by hot injection, with composition confirmed by XPS and ICP, applied to solar cells. Keywords: quantum dots, hot injection, gradient alloy, colloidal synthesis. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/CIHS2GAT)
- **[Molybdenum (Mo) back contacts for CIGS solar cells](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2053498)** — Ma et al. (2013). Review of Mo back contacts: resistivity, reflectance and adhesion all depend strongly on deposition parameters, and a two-step bilayer process gets low resistivity and good adhesion together. Keywords: molybdenum, bilayer process, adhesion, resistivity. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/M6U7S586)
- **[Technological status of Cu(In,Ga)(Se,S)₂-based photovoltaics](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.solmat.2013.08.030)** — Reinhard et al. (2013), *Solar Energy Materials and Solar Cells*. Selective overview of where chalcopyrite CIGSSe technology stood — absorber routes, device architectures and the efficiency records. The orientation piece for the whole page. Keywords: CIGS, chalcopyrite, technology status, absorber. Related: [[PV_Cell_and_Module_Technologies]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/3R6JZMIR)

Related: [[PV_Economics_and_LCA]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/GJF2T9WE)
