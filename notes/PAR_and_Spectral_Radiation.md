# PAR and Spectral Radiation

**Summary**: Photosynthetically active radiation and the spectral side of solar radiation — quanta versus energy, PPFD and PAR:global ratios, spectral distribution, and the instruments that measure it.

- **[Estimation of Global and Diffuse Photosynthetic Photon Flux Density under Various Sky Conditions Using Ground-Based Whole-Sky Images](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs11080932)** — Yamashita & Yoshimura (2019), *Remote Sensing*. Derives global and diffuse PPFD from whole-sky camera images, addressing the fact that diffuse PPFD is needed for photosynthesis modelling on short timescales but is rarely measured. Keywords: whole-sky imaging, diffuse PPFD, sky conditions, short timescale. Related: [[Solar_Radiation_Modelling]]. [zotero](zotero://select/library/items/DCZRS7P6)

Photosynthesis is more efficient per unit of diffuse than direct PAR, yet diffuse PAR is seldom measured; this tests four standard decomposition models against measurement. The direct answer to "which PAR decomposition should I use". Keywords: diffuse fraction, PAR partitioning, model evaluation, Erbs and Gu models.
